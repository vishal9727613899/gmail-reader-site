Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to Gmail Reader ("Application"), developed and operated by Vishal Thakkar ("Service Provider").

Purpose of the Application

Gmail Reader is designed to detect selected notifications received in the user's Gmail account, including notifications related to Google services and Google Console, and deliver corresponding notifications to the user's Telegram account.

Google Account Data We Access

When the user authorizes the Application, Gmail Reader may access Gmail data necessary to identify relevant messages. Depending on the permissions granted, this may include message metadata such as sender, subject, date, and message content required to create the Telegram notification.

How We Use Google User Data

Google user data is used only to provide the core functionality of Gmail Reader: identifying relevant emails and sending the requested notification to Telegram. The Application does not use Google user data for advertising, profiling, or unrelated purposes.

Sharing of Data

Gmail Reader may transmit selected information from relevant Gmail messages to Telegram solely to deliver the notification requested by the user. Data is not sold to third parties.

Data Storage and Retention

The Application processes data only as necessary to provide its functionality. Any temporary processing or retention is limited to operational needs. Gmail Reader does not use Gmail message data to build advertising profiles or for unrelated analytics.

Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect credentials, tokens, and data processed by the Application from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

Revoking Access and Data Deletion

Users can revoke Gmail Reader's access to their Google Account at any time through their Google Account security and third-party access settings. Users may also request deletion of data associated with the Application by contacting vishal9727613899@gmail.com.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Gmail Reader's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Third-Party Services

Gmail Reader uses Google services to access authorized Gmail data and Telegram to deliver notifications. The use of those services may also be subject to their respective privacy policies and terms.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
