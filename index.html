Gmail Reader

About Gmail Reader

Gmail Reader is a private utility that helps deliver selected email notifications from Gmail to Telegram.

Purpose

The application is used to monitor Gmail for relevant notifications, including messages related to Google services and Google Console, and send corresponding notifications to the user's Telegram account.

How it works

The user authorizes access to their Google account. Gmail Reader processes only the email data required to detect relevant messages and create the requested Telegram notification.
